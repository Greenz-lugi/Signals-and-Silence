A sound sensor detects sound waves and converts them into electrical signals to measure sound intensity (loudness) or volume levels.

Unlike the ultrasonic sensor, a sound sensor measures the sound's volume from the sound waves. Unlike ultrasonic sensors using ultrasonic
sound waves to figure out/find a distance like echolocation, a sound sensor's kinda like us humans listening, whispering, or yelling
and being able to hear/notice that.
